---
name: formal-literature-search
description: Run formal database searches with independent counts and exports.
---

# Call when

Call for systematic review, meta-analysis, guideline-grade, or other formal
review retrieval.

# Do not call when

Do not call to replace a commercial database whose credentials are unavailable,
or to silently treat discovery/challenger results as formal counts.

# Required outputs

Every source gets its own query, timestamp, count, raw export, source role, and
status (`AUTH_REQUIRED` when access is missing).
