# Systematic-review-meta template

Use this workspace for systematic reviews and meta-analyses. Keep the protocol,
search log, deduplication and screening decisions auditable. `privacy_level:
restricted` is the default for any non-public extraction or correspondence.
Do not fabricate study data, citations, registration IDs, or effect estimates.
Record the unit of analysis, effect measure, uncertainty, heterogeneity,
small-study and risk-of-bias decisions, and provenance for every result.
