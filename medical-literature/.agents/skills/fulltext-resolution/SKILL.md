# Call when

Call after paper normalization and verification when an abstract is
insufficient for screening or extraction.

# Do not call when

Do not upload private Zotero PDFs to unknown services, bypass paywalls, or
redistribute licensed full text.

# Current boundary

The V2 core records the interface and privacy policy; vendor full-text
providers remain `OPTIONAL_UNVERIFIED` until locally smoke-tested.
