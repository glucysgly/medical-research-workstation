# MR-GWAS template

Use this workspace for Mendelian-randomization or GWAS-derived analyses. Public
summary data may be classified `public` only after verification; participant
level or controlled-access data remains `restricted`. Raw inputs are immutable.
Predefine exposure/outcome, instruments, statistical unit, estimand, ancestry,
linkage disequilibrium, pleiotropy and sensitivity rules. Distinguish causal
assumptions from observed association and record provenance for every dataset.
