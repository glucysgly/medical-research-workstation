# Shared statistical-unit contract

Declare the biological inferential unit before analysis. Technical replicates
must be collapsed or modeled according to the protocol; they must not inflate
the biological sample count. In single-cell workflows, cells are observations
within donors or samples unless a defensible design explicitly says otherwise.

Every result should state the unit, aggregation rule, uncertainty method and
limitations. The public framework does not choose a unit merely to increase
significance.
